07 PHYLOGENETICS · POPULATION GENETICS COMPLETED

Indian gaur phylogenetics

Master's thesis at the Central University of Kerala, supervised by Dr. M. Nagarajan. A study of genetic diversity in the Indian gaur (Bos gaurus) across four states, using the mitochondrial cytochrome b gene. I did all of the work except the sequencing itself, which an external agency ran.

Result Twelve sequences gave 8 haplotypes and formed a single clade, with mithun (Bos frontalis) as the closest relative and domestic cattle clearly separate. The limits are one mitochondrial locus and a small sample, because most field DNA was too degraded to amplify.

  • MEGA
  • Arlequin
  • focusedMDS
  • PCR
Master's thesis, August 2022

05 / CAPABILITIES & LIMITS AN HONEST INVENTORY

Things I can do,
and things I can't.

Can

  • Build a reproducible pipeline from raw reads to a defensible figure.
  • Read a methods section and reimplement it without emailing the authors.
  • Diagnose why a model failed and say so plainly.
  • Write code that someone else can run six months from now.
  • Work at the bench: axolotl regeneration experiments at UNAM, and DNA extraction and PCR on degraded field samples for my master's thesis.
  • Sit with an ambiguous biological question until it becomes a testable one.

Can, with caveats

  • New wet-lab systems. Axolotl, yes. Anything else, give me the protocol and I will read it twice.
  • Deep learning at scale. I know the mathematics; I have rarely had the GPUs.
  • Statistics. I know when to ask a statistician, which is most of the time.
  • Cluster work. I will read the documentation first and queue politely.

Cannot

  • Make your data clean. Handling the mess is the job.
  • Make a result significant.
  • Tell you which of the 400 parameters broke it without looking.
  • Promise a finding before I have seen the data.
  • Give up without trying.

The last one is the only entry on this page I am actually proud of. Everything above it is a boundary I would rather state than have you discover later.
